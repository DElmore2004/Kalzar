A town that rests within a bubble created by a [[Fold Anomaly]]. About halfway into the [[Steelclaw Bay]], if a ship is oriented correctly, and at the correct speed, they will tip into the ocean, sailing down into it's depths. The path will reject and not submerge vessels that have improper headings. At the end of the path, there will be an underwater waterfall at the mouth of a cave. Passing through the waterfall, you are exposed to air and a bustling town, primarily consisting of Galapa folk. The cave ceiling has numerous crystals embedded in them that simulate a day night cycle, the houses and buildings are all built with stained glass rooves with shutters to close them off for privacy, but double as lighting. [[The Horseman of War/PC's/Smith/Archelon|Archelon]] is a trade and cultural hotspot. Being primarily a culture of [[Grave Speakers]], the town trains in many fields, from artisan, to combative, to religious trades. The city ~~has~~ had a Tomb of Rest, and a Wake of Memories (a graveyard for those that did not earn their way into the Tomb of Rest, such as criminals, people who died outside of the culture, etc.). [[The Horseman of War/PC's/Smith/Archelon|Archelon]] ~~is~~ was a happy town, there ~~is~~ was a council of elders that spearheaded the decision making. This caused some discourse amongst newer generations, as the elders seemed to live significantly longer than average Galapa lifespans.
