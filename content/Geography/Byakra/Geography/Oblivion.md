Oblivion is a honeypot trap. It is the loveliest place you can happen to stumble into. Anyone that steps foot close to the town is enraptured by it's spell. They are drawn to the town by the feelings associated with their happiest memories. Anyone that spends time here quickly lose exterior motivations, and begin to build happy lives within the town. Sometimes, people slip out of Oblivion, and if they do, they cannot actively recall memories of their lives there, only glimpses in dreams and daydreams. Some of the residents know that Oblivion is a strange place, and that some people are destined to come and go. 
Leaving is always a sad thing, but sometimes it must be so.
