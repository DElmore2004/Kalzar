The leyline separating the Jenmu Ocean between the continents has many names. The Leyline, The Great Barrier, The Magekiller Wall, but religious scholars have linked the divinity within the leyline to the creator of this world, [[The Silver Light]]. 
This barrier has many properties still not discovered by many scholars. But it's primary function is to retether the world to it's cosmic anchor. Within [[The Beyond]], there is an endless expanse, and all components of Kalzar would drift endlessly through it. To keep the world together and minimize spatial fluctuation through endless space, an anchor was made, wrapping around the world. A divine embrace from a presence beyond space.
This does create side effects, and this is what the denizens of Kalzar believe to be the functions of the Silver Commandment. Because of the intense magical output of this barrier, any external magical effect is nullified forcefully. Due to how magical items and artifacts are constructed, the material barrier containing the magical effect within the item is usually enough for there to be no real effect on magical items. However this is not 100% and crews usually compile their magical items together in a cell for safety. Some very powerful magical artifacts, if brought across the extent of the leyline, can bring the bearer very painful shocks, the material casing of the artifact is not a sufficient enough physical barrier for the leyline's effect. And so the artifact discharges the excess magical energy into the wielder to very painful effect. 
Creatures and magical persons of power may have a very strong reaction to the barrier. The constant stream of arcane energy can overload these creatures or people. Upon contact or even proximity to the Commandment, these entities may enter a state of shock or paralysis, where the arcane overloads their senses. Some legends say that some of these powerhouses train and live near the barrier to gain it's insights.
