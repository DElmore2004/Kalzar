Around 800 years ago and prior, [[the Shards of Eorza]] were whole. The continent was a geologic hotspot and numerous volcanoes sprouted and formed a web of landmass that gradually filled with sediment. Eorza was incredibly fertile and the first peoples that settled on the continent opted for a very nomadic life. The culture instilled in every native to Eorza is a mutualism with the land, as well as a respect given to it, as there is an understanding that the land will only provide if treated well. 

Eorzan people decided very early to dedicate their lives to study, whether that be in art, craftsmanship, or scholastic. (As magic was not discovered when Eorza was whole, they were not mages, but this school of thought did apply when magic was discovered).

Eorza was shattered 9 different times during [[The Titanomachia]], as [[The Leviathan, Verizdao|Verizdao]] and [[Yokrot, The Blind General|Yokrot]] fought a bloody battle over the continent. The first break separated the top layers of the continent from the continental shelf of Byakra. The second and third are not exactly "breaks", but large impacts that shifted the continent south. The fourth break shattered the continent vertically into three different pieces. The last 4 breaks just shattered those sections into the archipelago that it is today.
