Verizdao guards [[the Shards of Eorza]]. He fought with [[Yokrot, The Blind General]] almost 800 years prior to modern day, as [[Yokrot, The Blind General|Yokrot]] assailed [[Eorza]] (which was whole at the time), the result of their fight was the shattering of Eorza into what it is seen as today. Verizdao has 11 heads, previously 13, but two have shown no signs of regrowing since the fight with [[Yokrot, The Blind General|Yokrot]]. He is a deep crimson color, so that underwater he appears black. Piercing blue eyes line the necks of the 11 heads. No one has seen where the heads coalesce. He has unsurpassed mastery of ancient magic, legends say that he resides in an observatory/library deep beneath the surface of the ocean.
