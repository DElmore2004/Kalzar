The Grey Rose is an explorer's guild of sorts. Producing the first world map is the major source of their income and connections. They send travelers around the world to update and collect topographic and diplomatic information. This gives them a lot of privilages across different factions. Grey Roses typically travel alone or with one other companion maximum. They are also found with their claws around undercities, dealing in smuggling and black market information. If you find one, they may have an artifact or two that could be of interest to you...
