The Grave Speakers are a culture of necromancers that originated in the [[Peaks of Usrailak]] at the northern point of Yindrilan. 

The legend of their origin is constantly debated by [[The High Tombs]] and Speaker scholars, for different reasons, but the general conversation is to be occasionally wary of depending on who you speak with. 

Around 250 years ago, [[Harlaxi, the Bloodweave| Harlaxi of Clan Iceheart]] roamed the glaciers north of the tundra of the [[Peaks of Usrailak]] when he stumbled and fell, his boot clipping a frozen tome beneath the snow. Upon trying to dislodge it, he discovered the owner of the book, a large humanoid corpse, frozen in ice underneath the snow, and the corpse refused to let go of the book. Luckily, [[Harlaxi, the Bloodweave|Harlaxi]] was born with very simple arcane capabilities, so he gathered some wood from a tundra about a half day's journey away and started a fire in the middle of this glacial plane. The corpse thawed to reveal black horns and sharp features. When the corpse thawed fully, he attempted once again to dislodge the tome from the bony hand. To his incredible surprise, the corpse suddenly asked him to let go of his tome, and that asking would suffice. 

[[Harlaxi, the Bloodweave|Harlaxi]] discovered that the body belonged to a the white dragon, [[Vurusalk]]. Even though the dragon was dead, it had laid a spell over it's body to reanimate, though it withheld the knowledge of sentient reanimation. [[Vurusalk]] taught [[Harlaxi, the Bloodweave|Harlaxi]] necromancy and the responsibility of being a necromancer. The ethical controversies created by societal norms surrounding death. The following years, as the spell on [[Vurusalk]] waned, [[Harlaxi, the Bloodweave]] created the Grave Speakers, a society of necromancers and their families that dedicated their lives to be an organic trove of knowledge. They write none of their history, passing knowledge through the art of storytelling. 

Not all Grave Speakers are necromancers, in fact, today, many are not. Some are warriors, mages of other craft, bards, druids, or even clerics or seraphs. Many Grave Speakers beyond that are just people that wander and learn, cartographers, craftsman, artists. But almost all are storytellers.

Grave Speakers have locations and sites all over Kalzar. They have tombs and catacombs called Tombs of Rest. Where after a Grave Speaker dies, another Grave Speaker performs [[The Rites]] on the body, decapitating it, to then it is sent off to be prepared and sealed for preservation in the Tombs of Rest.
