An information guild that operates within the context of honorary duels. They are all skilled swordfighters that live for display. During their duels, other members of their guild trade and collect information. They commonly display themselves depicting some sort of multicolored flame that appears to dance the longer you look at it. 

The guild leader is actually an Elder Oblex, a mimic ooze that absorbs the memories and can shapeshift into those it has consumed. Meaning if someone won't talk, and with the right permissions, there is no willpower the Iridescent Flame cannot burn.

They operate primarily out of Wystala, that is where the leader resides, but they can be found in most upper social circles.
