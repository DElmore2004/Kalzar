The second and last of the Trials. A Dormant must willingly take this trial. Despite the overlying purpose of these trials, many Dormant do not survive this last trial. [[Iliak 'Nuam]], the Guardian of [[the Black Gates]] takes the Dormant under her wing, preparing their mind, body, and soul for the Trial. After some time, whenever she deems them ready, she splits their soul into 93 different incarnations. This is an incredibly painful process. Most that go through this portion of the trials scatter. Never to reach any afterlife, dispersing into the [[The Beyond]]

These incarnations, known as **Eyes of the Mist**, depart to the [[Nightmare Wastes]], to fight their endless battle. When an Eye of the Mist dies, the soul, if not devoured and consumed by some concoction of Nightmare creature, seeks another of it's soul shards to bind to and regrow, when it has grown and recovered to an adequate strength, the soul shatters again and fights once more.
