Seraphina, Dormant Champion of [[Iliak 'Nuam]]. Known as the Witch of Blade and Soot.
Seraphina is [[Azazel]]'s sister. They awakened at a young age from the prodding of [[Hastios, The Baron of Insanity]]. They wielded golden arcane gauntlets that they left behind before their departure through [[The Black Gates]].
Seraphina is a little taller, around 5'10", long brown hair (now black), she had bright golden eyes that shined with hope and a curious nature. She wore bright clothing and always questioned the world around her. She was gifted in every aspect, hand to hand combat, arcane arts, and book studies. 
In her [[The Awakened|Awakening]], [[Hastios, The Baron of Insanity|Hastios]], prodded her to ruthlessly kill her target. "It must be done, you have no other way to survive", and "It will be the hardest thing you have ever done, but it is exactly what you have been preparing for, all this time..." She had to kill an orphaned boy that had just gotten his start in life as a Byakran soldier. His name was [[Timmet of Wystala]]. With tears in her eyes and utter frustration, as he pleaded for his life, she plunged the longsword she had taken from his waist in combat, into his heart, killing him.

Becoming [[Dormir|Dormant]] changed Seraphina. She lost her spark. Life became dull and bleak. She retreated back to [[the Dreamlands]] where she found her family gone, especially her adored sister, [[Azazel]]. She felt abandoned and lost, with not even a note depicting where they went. She went on a voyage through [[the Black Gates]]! Did they not believe or trust in her! [[Hastios, The Baron of Insanity|Hastios]] told her everything she needed to hear. "They mourned for a little while, but everyone moves on. Just as you must now". The only thing left when she returned was an old flame, [[Corvaux]]. A childhood best friend that she now realized what it meant to have someone care for you. He welcomed her back with the same passion he had years ago. But she knew that he only cared for who she was, not who she is now. Her motivation for returning backfired, becoming a blade of it's own that plunged Seraphina into despair. 
Seraphina crawled back to [[the Black Gates]], the only real purpose she had in life. Where [[Iliak 'Nuam]] sat waiting. As a champion and [[Trial of the 93 Eyes|Eye of the Mist]], she defends [[the Dreamlands]] from scions of nightmares and Nightmare spawn of [[Tartourak- The Pit of Whispers and Madness]]. She has earned herself a title within the [[Nightmare Wastes]], as the Witch of Blades and Soot fights, death spurs fire around her from the corpses, and the flash of a dark sword cuts through, extinguishing that fire. Ever vigilant in her defense, she pivots her emotion into her sole purpose in life. Perpetuating the cycle: Fight->wounded->recover->shatter. That is the terrible purpose of the Eye.
