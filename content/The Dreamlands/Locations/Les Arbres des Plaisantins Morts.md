The Trees of Dead Jesters are where those who fall to madness find themselves Tucked into [[the dreamlands]], this forest is around any corner. Some believe that dead laughing gods are buried under it's damp soil. Dark wood trees that occasionally have bark twisted into clownish grins. The canopy hides the ground from the stars. It is a dark and gloomy place. Mist and fog drifts across the ground, it's tendrils curling around the base of trees. An off putting feature of this area is that there are many dilapidated fountains scattered through the forest, some overtaken by the woods, others that just barely work, spewing clear water. You never find anyone here, only the laughing of trees creaking in the wind.
