## Hall of Broken Reflections

Initial text of the handout

## To Fight is to Hurt and Break
The purpose of this puzzle is to identify **content**.

Initial text of the handout:
* The bleak surroundings only display a dark, glossy floor, with mist swirling just above the surface until only darkness is visible in the distance. In front of you is a small pile of glass shards. There appear to be words carved into them.
* Written into the floor before you:
	* To Fight is to hurt and break. The broken thoughts stored here were never completed. Completion was considered unsafe.

___
Carved into the shards of glass:
* Dreams are lies
* Necessity demands tradition
* Denial is a form of preservation
* The gods sleep also
* Ignorance is kindness
* Two lives share one absence
* What is sacrificed continues in function

---
**Instructions**:
The correct thoughts do not contradict.
They support a *system*.

---
**Answer**:
1. Denial is a form of preservation
2. Two lives share one absence
3. What is sacrificed continues in function

**Hints**:
1. Your answer is finished in 3 components
2. What benefits the system

## Tombstones of Forgotten Sacrifices
The purpose of this puzzle is to identify **voices**.

Initial text of the handout:
* The bleak surroundings only display a dark, glossy floor, with mist swirling just above the surface until only darkness is visible in the distance. In front of you are 3 stone plinths.
	* Before you are the Tombstones of Forgotten Sacrifices. Some truths collapse when spoken alone. Others collapse when spoken by many.

___
**Plinth A**
*The surface is unmarred.  
Dust never settles.*

When one voice is imagined here, the space feels complete.
When more are imagined, the air dulls and flattens.

---
**Plinth B**
*The stone is fractured cleanly down the middle.  
Each half resonates faintly.*

A single imagined voice echoes endlessly, unresolved.
Two imagined voices overlap, and the echo stills.

---
**Plinth C**
*The surface is crowded with shallow impressions, none distinct.*

No single imagined voice lingers.
When many are imagined together, the space hums, unified.

---
**Instructions**:
The Vault does not care who speaks.
Only *how many agree*.

**Answer**:
Speak alone
Speak in a pair
Speak together
