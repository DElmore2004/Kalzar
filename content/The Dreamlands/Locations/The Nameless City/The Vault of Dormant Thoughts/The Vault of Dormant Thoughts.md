The Vault of Dormant thoughts is one of the very few truly restricted areas in the [[Zultraxi Academy]]. There are many false entrances, faux rooms, and security personnel (Such as [[Corvaux]] and [[Laundel]]) guarding this vault. The vault contains information, spells, and artifacts that only select few are allowed to know. Only [[the Founder]] knows all of what is within the Vault.
