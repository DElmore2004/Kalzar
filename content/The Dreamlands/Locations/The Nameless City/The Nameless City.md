The Nameless City is the "only" city in [[the Dreamlands]]. It disappears into any horizon you look off into. The buildings shift and twist in angles that should not be able to still be standing, the streets can twist upwards and ripple like a river, children can be seen running into an alleyway and coming out across the street from where they entered. Direction is meaningless here, the city operates off of a psuedo-dream-logic. There are notable locations such as the [[Zultraxi Academy]], [[Les Arbres des Plaisantins Morts]], [[The Bright Ports]], [[The Bazaar Between Time]]
