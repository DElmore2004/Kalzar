The sole magic academy in [[the Nameless City]]. 
The academy is a very unique place, many believe that the facility itself has a mind of it's own. 
Nestled within [[the Dreamlands]], towering over [[the Nameless City]], the Zultraxi Academy of the Arcane. White bricks of marble construct large spires, roofed in turquoise shingles. What people first notice are what seem like small strips of paper darting through the air between windows. The main entrance of the academy are these large double copper doors that only open for prospective students, remaining shut most of the time. Throughout the 8 different spires over the academy, representing the 8 different schools of magic: Transmutation, Evocation, Necromancy, Divination, Enchantment, Illusion, Abjuration, and Conjuration, they have different appearances corresponding to their schools. Transmutation has an oscillating appearance, the spire shifts between composition frequently, as golds, silvers, obsidian, and stone ripples across the surface, a blue flame sits atop the spire. At the evocation tower, elemental forces writhe up the spire, converging to a sphere at the spire's zenith. At the Necromancy Tower, the spire itself is made entirely of bone, as a spectral winged snake spirals around the spire, converging upon a raging orange flame. The Divination Tower is the shortest, made of glowing marble, purple drapes hang from outlooks around the tower, a radiant warmth emanates from the top of the spire. The Enchantment tower is the most sporadic, changing it's shape, staircases whirling at it's exterior a purple and yellow arcane eye peers down from the peak of the spire. The Illusion tower has numerous illusory creatures and battles occurring around it, flying soldiers firing arrows through the tower, mages shooting beams through the soldiers, the top of this tower is seemingly missing, however is just invisible. The Abjuration tower is split horizontally at the middle, floating dozens of meters above the lower half, connected by a large portal between the two halves, The top looks almost like a sentry tower. Lastly, the conjuration tower is very different, it only creates the area needed at the moment, so you find students walking one stair at a time, classrooms floating in space, needed in that moment. In the middle of the academy, a large courtyard with practice arenas and crafting stations, the academy is constantly bustling with activity.

At a young age, those with an aptitude for the arcane find themselves passing it on the street more and more frequently during their day to day. One day, as they look upon the copper doors, they open, and are invited inside. From that day onward, they are students of the institution. They learn how to understand and interpret the arcane. Even those not of magical talent occasionally are accepted into the school, for their deep capability to understand and pick up magical trades. 
The School sponsors the upkeep up [[The Bazaar Between Time]]. The School also houses the Vault of Dormant Thoughts
