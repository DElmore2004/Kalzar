Within some corner of [[the Nameless City]], an endless layout of docks, bays, ships, schooners, fishing stores, anything you can *dream* of a port having is here. Characterized by the crystalline formations that are scattered and provide lighting and infrastructure to the location. These crystals take many forms, some almost looking like fire licking up the sides of buildings, some like glowing pearls beneath the waves, and others like explosions lighting up fountains. The docks themselves house many different sizes of ships, and an incredibly complex network of passages and streamers hang over the water for nautical directions.
