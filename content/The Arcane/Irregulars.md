Irregulars are individuals that, for a myriad of reasons, interact with [[The Fold]] differently. Irregulars have such a strong world view that it impacts the world around them. Often formed by trauma, this worldview is so potent that their will is carried across into the space around them and within them. There are many different ways Irregulars are "created", these are the methods that have been documented:
1. A Vow with enough conviction.
	If an Irregular vows that magic shall never hinder them again, perhaps due to a loved one dying while the Irregular was magically restrained. If there is *excess* conviction and will behind the vow, and that conviction is actively upheld (often subconsciously), then magic will refuse to restrain that Irregular.
2. Worldview
	This is much more difficult to identify and even more so to replicate. Irregulars with an altered worldview inherently *know* that the world works **their** way. And they impose this will onto the surrounding area. For example, an Irregular that was harmed as a child by a mage could disassociate reality enough by believing that they have the power to retaliate, and that they are impervious to magic. If by chance they become an Irregular, they could perhaps steal the magics of those that attack them with the arcane, taking no damage themselves.
