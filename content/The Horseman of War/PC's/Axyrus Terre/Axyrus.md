Axyrus Terre is a Ribbet, born and raised in [[Croaksville]], located somewhere beneath the [[Nezdrait Swamp]]. [[Croaksville]] is small mining village in the underborne, home to no greater than fifty residents. The residents of [[Croaksville]] are taught from a young age to work in the mines, each having different roles. They are expected to master their chose tool, for Axyrus it was a sledgehammer. Axyrus spent most of his time in Croaksville smashing boulders into smaller boulders which could be further processed into ores, such as copper, iron, and coal. All of the Ribbets of [[Croaksville]] are very strong as a result of spending their time in the mines. Axyrus had been growing tired of the mines for some time, anxiously thinking that there’s more to life than smashing rocks. One day Axyrus decided that he had spent enough time in [[Croaksville]], and traversed his way out of the underborne. Weeks before he left the village Axyrus began to prepare. Selling his belongings in order to buy some basic full plate armor and have his sledgehammer polished up. Once that was taken care of all he needed was his beloved sledgehammer, his armor, rations, other basic supplies, and his trusty pipe. Axyrus, along with the other Ribbets in [[Croaksville]] had the tendency to smoke pipes, tobacco was one of the few plants that was accessible to [[Croaksville]] via trade. There is no farming or cultivation in [[Croaksville]], only rock. The journey from [[Croaksville]] to the surface takes roughly three days for its residents, but longer for those who cannot see in the dark. On his journey Axyrus thought of returning to his village, but the thrill of exploration kept him going.
