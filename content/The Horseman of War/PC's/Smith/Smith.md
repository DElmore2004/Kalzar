Within this loreborn tribe age is determined by successful adventures/discoveries. 
With the elders having extended natural life due to “magic”. This tribe has advanced knowledge of the world with new discoveries being rare to the elders.

[[Archelon]] is a society that comes from an extension of the grave speakers. They have a few similarities including resurrecting their heads to commune and teach the living about the world.

Within [[Archelon]], knowledge is power, every successful voyage must be recorded then presented to the elders to determine if your discovery/adventure is worthy enough to advance your age. Then it is a question of how much your age is advanced.

Naturally younger [[Archelon|Archelons]] tend to base their discoveries and adventure on learning weaponsmithing and fighting techniques. Naturally middle age [[Archelon|Archelons]] tend to begin to metal with the arcane, safely through artifacts. Then naturally older [[Archelon|Archelons]] desperately seek out the secrets of the arcane, naturally leaving only the elders who successfully learn the secrets to natural life extensions.

The elders are an odd bunch, these Galapas have found a secret to immortality and unlike most knowledge found on quests this secret is wisely guarded. Overall the elders don't have much in common, they are different types of Galapas, they are different sizes, shapes, and all have different preferences and ideals. The one thing they all have in common is that they all  left on longer journeys early on in their life. The elders don't number like the rest of the tribe, right now there are only five. Unlike most [[Archelon]], the elders don't do very much. Every once in a while we see them publicly judge the aging of an [[Archelon]], besides that they spend most of their time within the temple where very few are allowed access. (just elders and close family) 

Most dream of glimpses of the riches and luxury that lie within that temple. The secret is kept well, even the family of elders don't share what lies behind the temple gates.     

Smiths log,  journey 000 

Today I just turned eight years old. I have hit the books hard like any wanna be elder would but it has become clear that i cant advance any further by just studying here. I have read all the classics and spoken with all of the authors. This has motivated me to go on an adventure of my own. It won't be easy but I have learned well from those who spoke before me, nothing worth doing is ever easy. For now I have set off into The Gloom Woods, it may be a challenge but I am ready, with just rations, and my new camping set I will not be stopped until I learn the secrets of this world.   


Smiths log, journey 001 

It has been a rough journey. The Gloom Woods are no joke, fortunately I was prepared, those that came before me warned me of this obstacle and told me how to hide myself. Fortunately my shell is dark, an advantage I will forever cherish. However to escape a fierce foe I had to ditch some of my camping supplies, I had to lose my light and a few cooking supplies. It is now time for me to learn to adapt like the manny that came before. 


Smiths log, journey 005 

Things are getting rough, I have had to run and escape most things I've encountered. The only adventures I've had is slashing at mosquitoes with my camping knife, surprisingly I even hit a few. Maybe one day I will learn to master this technique. Anyway I have about crossed the blade fields. I have mostly dodged all sentient life. The only ones who notice me are the beasts that can smell my unusual stench.

Smith Background

Smith is on a long journey attempting to master weapon forgery. Unlike most of its kind Smith is attempting to travel with a group. Because for some unknown reason this [[Archelon]] forms attachments which is unheard of among this tribe. This difference makes Smith an outcast among his peers because compassion is madness in that it always leads to irrational thinking. 

Smith set out on his journey recently with the goal of learning about the world. Smith is currently trying to master smithing both weapons, armor, and eventually he wants to work on weapons that are powered by the arcane itself.
