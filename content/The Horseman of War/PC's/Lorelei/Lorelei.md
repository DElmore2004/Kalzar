**A Living Curse Born of the Sea, an Albatross Around Her Neck


117 years ago, [[Lady Odette]] and [[Captain Flint]] of the [[Hanged Man’s Fleet]] gave birth to Lorelei, the heir to the helm. On the Liberty of the Damned ship, which commonly inhabited the [[Jenmu Ocean]], elves like Lorelei gained skills that assisted in their plundering practices. Sailing, fencing, archery, balance and climbing, celestial navigation, and more, were just part of her everyday life. Lorelei was especially cunning and sly compared to the others in her fleet, often being recruited to sneak onto other ships in [[Steelclaw Bay]] to loot their spoils. Often flying under the radar, she made more friends with the shore animals than she did with fellow elves. When she was a child, a friendly Brandt’s Cormorant took a liking to her. They spent their days together snagging food from the port and tracing the constellations. When the fleet was weathering a storm and one of the crew became desperate for a meal, though, that was all over.
Losing her best friend was just the beginning of Lorelei’s affliction, but a voyage to the Sea of Stars changed everything. In the midst of her arranged marriage to [[Caspian]], son of the Quartermaster, Lorelei encountered many sleepless nights. She often heard many sounds of the ocean passing her by, but nothing had ever struck her like the melodic tune she heard one night. When she followed it, she found a beautiful siren sitting on the jagged rocks that protruded out of the water. Lorelei knew of the danger of sirens, but she found herself jumping in the water and swimming towards her regardless. She would learn that her name was [[Semaris]]. After visiting her incessantly, Lorelei fell deeper into her trance, replacing all she had ever known for her newfound devotion. She didn’t care that she was a siren. A rose by any other name would smell as sweet, she thought. Their connection grew stronger, and Lorelei felt herself becoming more powerful. Before she knew it, the water she spent her life fighting against was moving for her. She couldn’t explain it, nor the tattoo that appeared on her leg the night of their first kiss. As the date of her wedding grew nearer, she grew more resentment against her parents and longed to be with [[Semaris]] forever. It almost felt like a fever dream when she called upon the water to raise the siren into the window of her parent’s bedchambers, locking the door. Fourteen years later, the memory still wakes Lorelei from any sleep she might manage. The [[Hanged Man’s Fleet]] still thirsts for revenge after she stole the Liberty of the Damned, but she has no trouble outrunning the now ragtag group. She had feared the day that her powers would drain from her body, but it never came. Although, not a day goes by that she doesn’t seek a new host for them, longing to be rid of the connection to the siren that coerced her into committing unforgivable depravity and then abandoned her for fresh meat. She has since spent her days traveling the oceans of Kalzar, bringing aboard trustworthy travelers in hopes one of them might be able to free her from her search.
