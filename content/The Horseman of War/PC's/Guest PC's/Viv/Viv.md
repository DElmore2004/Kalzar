Born in [[The Floral Blanket]]. As a young child, he was chosen to be a caretaker of [[Bibliotheca Orbis]]. He was taken away from his family and was raised in the library to become a caretaker. Eventually (late teen equivalent) he realized this was not the life he wanted. He tried to leave, head caretaker denied it, so he ran away. He has not been back to the library since he left. He wandered until he found a group of rangers that took him in. This group taught him pretty much everything he knows. He traveled/wandered with this group until the “leader” of the group died. He then left the group wandered by himself, traveling around Byakra and Yindrilan. Tried to escape from a temporary war camp one night from being a soldier. Attempt failed, and he was punished — his wings were cut. He was left to die in war camp, as everyone moved on. He was then found by [[Ifnt]]/[[Smith]] and eventually made it to [[Ciralis]].
