The kid next door of [[Azazel]] and [[Seraphina]] growing up. [[Seraphina]] led him on for years, knowing he had a crush on her. She ranted to him about her views on [[the dreamlands]], and then she disappeared. This left him confused, as it went against all of the beliefs [[Seraphina]] preached. Corvaux joined the [[Zultraxi Academy]] to learn the arcane way of wizardry. The entire time, scrounging for information on [[the Dreamlands]] as a whole, what is the dirty little secret that makes the whole gear turn. In his time in the Academy, he worked his way into the right circles, becoming a good friend of [[Viralom Creshi]], the Head of Illusion and Hexes, who helped him into the position of guarding the [[Vault of the Dormant Thoughts]], a locked and restricted vault of spells, tomes, and history that is to be forgotten. To which they spend their job guarding to this day.

About 2 years ago, [[Seraphina]] shows up at his door. She looked very different, her brown hair now black, her bright golden eyes now reflected a dull grey. She was in a dark plate armor, her arcane gauntlets replaced with a dark runic blade. She looked like death. The last thing Corvaux expected was the hug he got, dropping the book he was reading on the ground.
Corvaux: "Where were you... you just vanished."
[[Seraphina]]: "I didn't have time to say goodbye. I completed the test of the Black Gate. I have to go soon, please don't make me talk to you about this."

She breaks the hug and rushes inside. She realizes that she doesn't know what to do with herself and just stands in the entrance to his living room.
S: "I went home. My folks weren't home, neither was [[Azazel]]."
C: "I can't speak to your parents. [[Azazel]] went missing about 3 years ago... I'm sorry.
[[Seraphina]] just stares into your face, sadness the only emotion present.
S: "Fuck. I was so close. If only I made my decision sooner maybe-. Okay. Sorry, I'm sure you have questions and I can only tell you so much I have to go."
C: "Wait why. You show up at my door after years. We didn't even have anything between us. I need an explanation. You can't just do that to me."
S: "...Fair. What do you know of the Pilgrimage."
C: "I know it's a façade, nothing more."
S: "That's more than most. Those that disappear go to another world. A mirror dimension of sorts, each and every one of us are linked to another person in that dimension. Every time you dream, and see another life, you are experiencing theirs."
C: "That doesn't make any sense."
S: "Well if you would let me finish. The Pilgrimage is to go to this other world, called Kalzar, and to kill your dream self."
C: "So that means your sister..."
S: "Most likely. I was trying to get back here in time. I didn't take the normal approach to the first test, pissed some powerful powers off. So it took me a long time to get back. And now I have to leave again. This time I will finish what I started."
C: "...That's a lot. Why are you here. Like, at my door."
[[Seraphina]] looks at Corvaux directly and takes a step forward.
S: "I don't know if I will be back this time. I wasn't able to say goodbye the first time... I needed to say it to someone. My trip made me realize things, about my time at home, and with you."
Seraphina looks out your window and her eyes widen.
C: "What is it?"
She rushes to the door and steps outside.
S: "I have to go. I love you. I'm sorry for everything."
She kisses Corvaux and before he can do anything, runs her hand along her sword, and disappears into a cloud of black feathers.
