An automaton was made in an ancient, long gone city by a craftsman who cared for their craft. It was a meticulously made machine with gears and systems that could be guided by the operator. Made of flowing metal and intricate designs, it was a work of art, but only in the hands of others, and once the city died, the art was lost. As the puppet lay in the ruins, a fungus crept in, drawn to the barren case full of desires to travel on its own terms, with the fungus full of desires to grow and give a true life. A new thing awoke, born with purpose and desire, and onward it has gone. They live their life, going varying paths and styles, and when their life ends, they rest as the fungi heal the body, and when the time comes, they wake up and start again. They leave behind the previous life, but bring with them the stories, for those are the lives of others, and the bits of their world that lives on.
