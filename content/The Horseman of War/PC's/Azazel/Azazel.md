Azazel is an infernis from [[The Dreamlands]].Much of Azazel’s early years are defined by her older sister, [[Seraphina]]. While Azazel was carefree and optimistic, her sister [[Seraphina]] kept her grounded with her constant questioning of [[The Black Gates]]. If not for [[Seraphina]], Azazel would have been perfectly content following the status quo, but instead her sister’s rebellious nature made Azazel a curious and confident young infernis. Although Azazel would never tell [[Seraphina]], she did believe all the things they taught them in school about writing and interpreting in your dream journal. And whatever else good little dream realm children do. Azazel lived a very simple life, only punctuated by her sister’s questions and arguments with their parents about what was really going on. Or really is was simple until one day (Azazel is probably late teens at this point) [[Seraphina]] disappears. The usual chatter of “oh well she must’ve gone on a journey to [[The Black Gates]]” came from Azazel’s friends, teachers, and even her parents. But Azazel knew better. [[Seraphina]] opposed everything that [[The Black Gates]] stood for, because they stood for nothing and were such a mystery. And more importantly, [[Seraphina]] never would have left without telling Azazel.


After [[Seraphina]]’s disappearance, Azazel stopped dreaming. For one night. For two nights, so she decided to say something to her parents. They thought this was strange, but my must have been due to her missing [[Seraphina]]. They told Azazel “it’s best to move on, your sister has just found her purpose”. After that, Azazel no longer told them about her lack of dreaming which continued on for an entire month. And she certainly didn’t tell them when she did start dreaming again. The same dream every night. Endless, black water. Azazel stood on a wooden structure atop these vast waters. In front of he was a woman with dark hair who looked frightened. Azazel saw her own hands, wearing [[Seraphina]]’s golden arcane gauntlets. The gauntlets showed Azazel’s own reflection, she wore a face of rage. And then the dream finished as Azazel releases a fury of magic that she didn’t even know she was capable of, killing the woman. This dream continued for months. Azazel spoke of it to no one, instead just became a little more quiet, and had a strong feeling that she was starting to go insane. She had never heard of someone in the dream realm having the same dream every night. This became her reality until one night, the dream was different. Instead of killing the woman and waking up in a cold sweat, Azazel found herself sitting in front of a chess board. A man sitting in front of her. [[Hastios, The Baron of Insanity]]s he called himself. (Idk what you want him to say to Azazel) Next thing Azazel knew, she woke up, surrounded by that vast, dark water. Only this time, there was no wooden structure beneath her. She was floating on top of a white feathered platform. But as Azazel tried to sit up, she realized that this was no platform. These were wings and they were attached to her
   
Azazel is unsure how to use her new wings so she continues to use them as a raft until she sees a huge wooden structure coming towards her. Before she has time to put too much thought into it she is taken aboard, there she comes face to face with the dark haired woman. Flashes of her dream flood her mind but she pushes them down, feeling repulsed by them. The woman is real? The dream couldn’t be real. There’s no way she could kill this woman. But anyway, [[Lorelei]] described her ship as kinda a refuge for lots of people, and Azazel becomes one of those refuges, learning the ways of the ship and getting to know [[Lorelei]]. The more Azazel learns, the more certain she is that she can’t kill her. But the nagging feeling she she’s going to have to can’t be shaken. Not sure if [[Hastios, The Baron of Insanity|Hastios]] would visit Azazel and say something to her that would spur her into thinking she has to kill [[Lorelei]], but either way Azazel decides one night that she’s going to do it, despite how close her and [[Lorelei]] have become. After all, she had seen it in her dreams, and dreams have power that can’t be ignored. Last second Azazel backs out, but obviously not before [[Lorelei]] knows that Azazel was going to try to kill her.
