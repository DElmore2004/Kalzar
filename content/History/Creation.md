In the beginning, there was [[The Silver Light]], a being of power. In it’s new life, it created the world we know and the laws of time, space, and creation. After spending it’s time expending its power to do these things, it fell into a deep slumber. In its slumber, a sliver of its power broke off, becoming a second being, a Primordial. This entity, a being of destruction and chaos, began to siphon power from [[The Silver Light]]. Using its power, it planted seeds of destruction and chaos across the sprouting world, becoming other primordials and elementals. The soft oceans became rougher, clouds darkened, and mountains filled with magma. Sensing a disturbance, [[The Silver Light]] awoke, only to find their world, grown a little… and a new entity. Overjoyed about the company, [[The Silver Light]] accompanied this new entity, known as [[Sycath]]. The two beings rejoiced at each other’s company. Though after time, certain scenarios didn’t add up, and even in the naivete of [[The Silver Light]], it began to piece things together. When confronting [[Sycath]], the primordial exposed a sliver of it’s plan and decided to attack [[The Silver Light]], elementals and other primordial beings poured from every crevice, [[The Silver Light]] retaliated out of rage and betrayal, this battle is known as the [[Cataclysm]], it split apart the world into 3 pieces, the Feywilds, the Shadowfell, and the Material Plane. After a long stalemated duel, [[The Silver Light]] found an opening and shattered [[Sycath]], the pieces scattering and forming the Elemental Planes of Fire, Water, Air, and Earth. Feeling exhaustion begin to overtake [[The Silver Light]], it’s final actions before slumber were to create security and authority over the world. It created the [[Mehala, the Golden Light]], a goddess over gods, and a Divine Realm to house her. [[The Silver Light]] instructed this being to oversee these worlds, do what it must, and destroy the threat that these new planes present, then [[The Silver Light]] fell into a long deserved slumber. The Golden Light, ever loyal to [[The Silver Light]], created the Prime Deities, and created the foundation for more deities to follow.
