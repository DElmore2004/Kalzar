Gods of the Pantheon get their power through worship as well as the existence of their portfolio, for example, a god of the sea exists as long as he has followers and the sea still exists. Gods can only come into existence when both conditions are filled, but can still exist if one condition disappears, but will be extremely weakened. Gods get their power by increasing these two factors, but a religious following has two factors of its own, quantity and quality. The amount of reverence or devotion a follower has makes a large difference in the amount of power their god has. This is how the more vile gods can still rival the power of the more known gods. Gods have the option of visiting the material plane to see their followers, but their strength is incredibly diminished outside of the divine realm due to the pact put in place by [[The Silver Light]] after the Second Awakening. This pact weakens all divine beings within any realm besides the Astral Sea or the Divine Realm, due to the god's infighting prior to the Second Awakening.
