Sycath, The Birth of Betrayal

As [[the Silver Light]] birthed the world in a cycle of [[creation]] and slumber, the abundant world energy as it would eventually be known, subsequently created a spark of life. Sycath broke off from [[the Silver Light]], looking over the infant Kalzar. Sycath was a bundle of primoridal energies and elements. Behind [[the Silver Light]]'s back, Sycath began [[creation]] of it's own. It created the elements, primordials, and the [[elementals]]. These functions wreaked havoc over the infant world, creating the initial reactions behind natural and arcane chemistry. When [[the Silver Light]] began to stir from it's slumber, Sycath ordered all of it's creations into hiding. Sycath was the first friend to the naïve [[The Silver Light|Silver Light]], a being above time and life. Sycath and [[the Silver Light]] created together for a long time, all while the primordial energies and entities morphed the world under the surface. However this would only last so long, as humans began to die from these primordials, [[the Silver Light]] began to notice. But it wasn't until Sycath plunged a cosmic equivalent of a knife in [[the Silver Light]]'s back, a concentrated burst of chaos into [[the Silver Light]]'s core. Chaos, destruction incarnate, blighted [[the Silver Light]], and in it's rage, caused the [[Cataclysm]]. Sycath was shattered in the [[Cataclysm]], and scattered across the realms. But it's legacy remains, the fear of primordials and the elements remain. The shards of Earth and Water have been sealed, but Death, Air, and Fire, still remain unchecked.
