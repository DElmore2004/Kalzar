God of Anguish- Tylirum, The Corrupted Violinist
Domains: Tempest, Grave
Divine Symbol: A crying, smiling face
*“Do you know what it means to have your life ripped from you, listen for a while, I shall ask again when I’m done”*
Tylirum was a musician that challenged the [[Baefrew, The Free Spirit]]. After a crushing defeat, the god of music soul cursed Tylirum. As Tylirum only played music that he stole, he made a living off of music and power he killed for and stole. The curse made it so any time Tylirum played music, it wracked him with immeasurable pain and guilt, and since it was inflicted on the soul, no resistance over time can accumulate. Having his life’s work torn forcefully from his grasp, his power causing immense pain, rather than finding new meaning, Tylirum embraced this thorned path, and bears a primal emotion of pure agony in its wake.
Tylirum wears a white mask, where these black holes peer into a black abyss where his eyes would be, and this smile curls up and sends slivers up to connect to the eyes, creating one large smile from eye to eye. He dresses quite formally, almost regally, in this dark blue noble-wear, silver shoulder pads and accents, black pants and shoes. He wears a rapier at his side, pitch black, that when unsheathed, turns into a violin bow or can remain a rapier. Either floating around him or within his white gloved hands resides a black violin, that seems to seep in the light of the surrounding area, seemingly alive and breathing, whenever music beings to play, this black smoke emanates from the violin, mask, the holes within the fingers of the gloves, and any opening in the clothing.
Tylirum is slightly mad, but only by definition, repetition expecting things to be different. There is no other way someone would willingly throw themselves so far into pain. Whenever Tylirum is not playing his instrument, he is quite calm, serene, calculative, but itching to be playing his instrument again. But when he does, he becomes quite cynical and psychopathic, anguish has taken hold over his body and mind and sends him on a destructive rampage, and lets just say his critics are… short lived.
You can find Tylirum in the concert halls of rising musicians, performing in very closed company within [[the Dreamlands]], or wandering the dreams of any in unbearable pain, or maybe just those with creative flair.
