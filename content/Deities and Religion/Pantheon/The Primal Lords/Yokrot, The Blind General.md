Goddess of Rage and War- Yokrot, the Blind General
Domains: War, Tempest
*“A moment of peace is time wasted, where words fall short, MY BLADE WILL PIERCE FARTHER, and you will not even feel your head fall to the ground.”*
Divine Symbol: A closed eye with a bolt of lightning streaking through it.
Yokrot is a being of pure rage personified, a dream given form, dreamt by [[Relzoura of the Mist]], fighting since it’s creation within [[the Dreamlands]]. Twisted by the dark powers that run rampant within [[the Dreamlands]]. Yokrot never was anything remotely mortal, a spirit of destruction from birth, and remains at that point to this day. She is technically not a god, but a being that has gathered so much power through warfare and killing, that her influence spreads and gains a following, much like others on this list. 
Yokrot resembles an incredibly fair woman, crimson red hair that reflects almost a lack of light, black almost. She wears a black blindfold that covers her eyes, she wears no armor, but instead prefers a cloth sports bra made of the pelt of some alien beast defeated within [[the Dreamlands]] and long leggings made of this same black material. She has decorated her clothing in blood of many colors, giving a new appearance every time she shows. Her body is of a tanned white, littered with many scars, of arrows and other piercings, swords and other slashes, and club and other beatings. Yokrot has never stopped fighting and never will. Underneath her blindfold, her skin is black as tar and her eyes are a matte red, but that does not hide the brimming rage and fire hidden behind the blank slate. None who sees her eyes live to tell the tale.
Yokrot has a very short temper and occasionally has outbursts mid sentence, she is by no means quiet and by all means ambitious and boisterous. She is quite open minded but by no means an idiot. Think twice before you challenge her in a battle of wits, for her strength may surprise you when unpredicted.
Yokrot searches out promising warriors, teaching them how to harness their rage and empower it, as well as furthering her own agenda of power. You will find her present in almost every large-scale battle, possessing soldiers or perhaps just watching, if not, scowering the Wastelands of [[the Dreamlands]], you will find her in an endless battle… no, massacre of many beasts of [[the Dreamlands]].
