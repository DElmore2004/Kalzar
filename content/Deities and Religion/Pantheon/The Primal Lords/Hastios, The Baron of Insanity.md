God of Madness- Hastios, the Baron of Insanity CE
Domains: Death, Trickery, Tempest
Divine Symbol: A large wide smile
*"I represent all the sins you never had the courage to commit, I am you, unbound by your chains"

Hastios was once mortal, like [[Tevara, Mistress of the White Cloth]], but a high elf with a long life span. Hastios is lawlessness and chaos personified. But his hyperintelligence can make it seem as if his insanity is an act, but sudden outbursts will make you think otherwise. Hastios constantly keeps people on their toes, wary of his next moves. He nourishes himself on the grief and sorrow caused by a broken heart, as well as the emotional trauma endured by the makings of insanity.
Hastios wears fine clothing of a dark crimson color. His coat has a short tail that extends downwards while silver buttons adorn the front accenting his pale skin and silver hair. His hair is kept up, but can extend to a little lower than his shoulders if left down, usually a strand or two leaks out from the braid on the right side of his face. A striking silver eye on the left carries a sharp gaze, while on the right, an ethereal green eye shakes with rage or madness, one cannot tell. He wears a black tuxedo style pants and noble wear on his feet of a silver color. His form seems to shift unnaturally, as if fading occasionally, due to his connection to [[the Dreamlands]].
[[Olokas]], the God of Murder, is an elven man with a burned and scarred face. His eyes vibrate with a maddening rage. He wears tattered noble robes, that actively burn and curl. He has long hair. All color is lost as he appears as a ghostly green apparition.
Hastios is very erratic, in everything from his actions to his speech, he will go from whispers to shouting at the top of his lungs. A very common theme of the Baron of Insanity is uncontrolled laughter. There are numerous cases of Hastios bursting into laughter for no apparent reason. But at the same time, to underestimated Hastios is a deadly mistake, his intelligence while mortal was unparalleled, and even amongst the gods, his scheming is on another level. Some think his insanity is a long ruse planned to worm his way into arcane studies of psyche, but the world will never know, not even Hastios.
You can find Hastios in numerous places, underground networks, inns, magical laboratories, whatever suits his fancy for the day. The one commonality of it all is, you don't go searching for Hastios, if you wish to find him, he will find you.
