Goddess of Gods- Mehala, The Golden Light- LG
Domains: Light, Knowledge, Arcana, Order
Divine Symbol: a 4 pointed golden star surrounded by smaller stars
Mehala, the Golden Light is the child of [[The Silver Light]], given the task of being the authority over the gods and to protect Kalzar and the realms,
Mehala is similar to [[The Silver Light]] in almost never accepting clerics, paladins or giving power in any way, but it’s now a conscious decision. Mehala only would end up doing such a thing in times of dire crisis, or to prepare for disaster, such as a Third Awakening of [[The Silver Light]]. 
Mehala is a very stern and authoritative figure, callhoused by years of putting unruly gods in check. She has a very maternal side to her as well, she cares for all the gods that have not turned their back on [[The Silver Light]] as if they were here children (and in a way, they are)
