God of Wealth and Commerce- Nozbach, The Jeweled Lord
Domains: Trickery, Knowledge, War
Divine Symbol: A damaged half crown, inlaid with the finest jewels
*“You can make anything a warfront, but the most dangerous ones are those without swords or spells, those will kill you and let you walk out of the building”*
Nozbach is the god of wealth and commerce, but he takes an atypical approach to this portfolio. Nozbach protects the two most important parts of trade in his eyes, the production and resource collection, as well as the marketplace. Nozbach gets his tactical prowess from the hardship presented in the commercial battlefront, full of sharks and thieves; as well as from the defense required on the resource gathering front, protection from bandits and magical beasts. War disciples of his go down routes of tacticians, while knowledge and arcana disciples follow his commandments and way of thinking to further study their fields.
Nozbach typically takes the appearance of a middle aged man with short black hair that reaches his eyes and bright emerald green eyes that carry a piercing gaze. A broken crown floats above his head, only half of it remains, and the rest of it is damaged. The crown itself is a fine gold inlaid with precious gems. He wears a robe of a dark royal purple, gold accents and designs are sprawled across his clothing, depicting ornate swords and gems, gold and riches. His pants are sleek black formal wear pants. And he wears a pair of shiny black tuxedo shoes. You will never find Nozbach without his long sword, whether he is learning on it, holding it, or even wielding it. The long sword, in comparison to his golden color scheme, is a base white in color, the sheath is a pearly white, almost iridescent, with gold accent swirling down the sheath, the blade itself is a spotless golden color, a handle guard of white, and the hilt being pitch black.
Nozbach is a very sly individual, in some circles he is known as the Devil of the High Heavens, cunning past even his age. His gaze is a look of pure control, everything you see is something he lets you see, and he knows that everyone is aware of that, yet he still comes out on top. When handling money, there is no one better than Nozbach. Nozbach is also a robin hood-esque figure, he only assists merchants that have been wronged or are at the bottom of the barrel. Nothing brings Nozbach more joy than bringing what was considered a “nobody” to greatness.
Nozbach can take many appearances outside his divine avatar, and he enjoys taking strolls through marketplaces or in mines watching future currency in the making.
