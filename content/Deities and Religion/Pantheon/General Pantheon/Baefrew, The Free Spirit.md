God of Music and Freedom- Baefrew, The Free Spirit CG
Domains: Arcana, Knowledge, Life
Divine Symbol: An eighth note surrounded with beams of light
*“Music is only a representation of the creative mind, so let it be heard! Reveille my friends!"*
Baefrew is freedom incarnate. He uses music to invoke emotion into those that hear his performances, giving them a sense of free spirit and inspiring them to go off and life their way of life. Baefrew, while a representation of free spirit, is incredibly prideful, wagering bets on almost any competition he partakes in. Which leads him to be a favorite within well partied circles, which his representation of free spirit is well welcomed in circles of wild folk, such as dryads and sprites.
