Goddess of Nature and Rebirth- Pyrisha, Caretaker of the Wild LG
Domains: Grave, Nature, Life
Divine Symbol: A flower blossoming atop an upside down embroidered mushroom
*“A new day represents everything that is good with this world, a fresh start brought by the new breeze.”*
Pyrisha is a goddess of life, but with the full knowledge and reasoning supporting life, being that something must fall for another to rise, forest fires rage across the land to enrich the soil and allow for further flora and fauna to compete with other organisms higher on the food chain. She understands life has another side to the coin, being death. She has an unrelenting hate for undeath, as anything that disrupts the cycle is a tarnish to a perfect state of being, and can and will break the folds of life and nature.
Depictions of Pyrisha describe her as an elegant woman, disheveled in her grace, a mushroom cap with small spores donned atop a head of long light lavender hair, silver glasses rest on a petite face, longer robes of greys, silvers, with patches of mushrooms and small shrubbery peaking out in various places.
Pyrisha visits her followers in various ages, typically in the age of one’s desires, for example, a young noble wishing for youth will see Pyrisha as a younger child. Her personality may seem very aloof at first, but closer analysis will see a very clear cut way of life. Pyrisha tends to get very informal and relaxed with her more trusted followers, even allowing a nickname of Pyri to be thrown around once or twice. She can be found more often than not in larger open fields of flowers, or residing within damp mushroom caves surrounded with different flora and fauna enjoying her company.
